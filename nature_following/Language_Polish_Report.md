## "RECOVERY" ARTIFACTS
- "The implemented recovered training branch..."
- "recovered training weights..."
**Problem:** These sound like internal project notes rather than a scientific paper. The reader doesn't care if the branch was "recovered."
**Fix:** Use "final trained policy" or "baseline configuration."
